# NearWake Play Store Listing Draft

This file is a working draft for store-facing copy.

Use it to keep the listing language aligned with the current product and the release-readiness notes.

## 1. Positioning

NearWake is not a general transit app.
It is an arrival-alarm app for people who are tired, distracted, commuting, or likely to miss their stop.

## 2. Candidate Short Description

Draft option 1:

> Arrival alerts and leave-by reminders for bus and train commutes.

Draft option 2:

> Wake before your stop with battery-aware trip alerts and reminders.

## 3. Candidate Full Description

Draft:

> NearWake helps you avoid missing your stop on bus and train commutes.
>
> Pick a destination, start a trip, and let NearWake monitor quietly in the background. When your stop is getting close, the app raises an alert before you miss it. For repeated commutes, NearWake can also learn typical departure patterns and suggest leave-by reminders.
>
> What NearWake focuses on:
>
> - arrival alerts for active trips
> - leave-by reminders for familiar commutes
> - battery-aware monitoring modes
> - repeat-trip convenience instead of repetitive setup
> - visible fallback behavior when permissions or signal quality reduce reliability
>
> What NearWake does not try to be:
>
> - a full route-planning platform
> - a live vehicle map
> - a ticketing app
> - an account-first service
>
> NearWake is designed to stay honest. Android permissions, device battery settings, and network conditions can affect reliability, and the app should surface those limits instead of hiding them.

## 4. Safe Feature Bullets Right Now

These match the current repo direction:

- destination-based trip setup
- route-aware monitoring when Google routing is available
- destination-only fallback when provider routing is unavailable
- leave-by reminders from learned commute patterns
- light, dark, and system appearance modes
- diagnostics and reliability guidance in-product

## 5. Claims To Avoid Until Field Testing Is Complete

Do not overstate these yet:

- "never miss your stop"
- "works perfectly while sleeping"
- "guaranteed locked-screen reliability"
- "minimal battery use on every device"
- "production-ready"
